# Roles & Permissions

Every SAM user is assigned one of three roles. Your role is shown in the user menu at the top right of the screen.

## Role overview

| Role | Who it is for |
|---|---|
| **Viewer** | Read-only access — can browse and search all data |
| **Editor** | Day-to-day management — can create and edit locations, assets, and attachments |
| **Admin** | Full access — manages users, system settings, and has all Editor capabilities |

Contact your SAM administrator to have your role updated. Administrators can change roles in **Administration → Users**.
